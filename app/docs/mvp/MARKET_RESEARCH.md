# Hivyss — Market Research & Commercial Strategy

> **Status:** Research captured **2026-06-13** (Steam/PC, Early-Access, indie scale).
> Source: a multi-agent deep-research pass (28 sources fetched, 124 claims, 25
> adversarially verified → **21 confirmed, 4 killed**) + two manual data points
> (RimWorld *Insectoids 2* mod, *The Battle Cats*). **All revenue/owner numbers
> are third-party ESTIMATES unless flagged "official"; gross ≠ take-home.**
> Treat genre-aggregate/mean figures as *context*, never as a forecast for one
> small indie (survivorship bias — every named comparable is a *survivor*).

---

## TL;DR (the headline)

**The niche is validated AND contested.** A near-identical direct competitor —
**Hive Blight** — already shipped your premise (insect-hive roguelite-autobattler
with mixable clans) and is **"Very Positive" (93% / 252 reviews)**. So:
- **Good:** appetite is *proven*; the theme + combat shape are de-risked.
- **Sobering:** you're now a *follower* in your own niche → win on
  **differentiation + execution**, not on being first.

**Hivyss's moat = exactly the systems we've been designing** — pheromone
*area-command*, the **Royal hero + positional economy**, the **in-battle swarm
economy**, **2.5D side-view**. Hive Blight has **none** of these (it uses
board/placement, no queen-hero, no pheromones). *The premise is taken; the verbs
are open.*

**Realistic outcome:** a **sustainable-but-modest** income is achievable *if*
well-differentiated + well-executed (review quality). A breakout (StS2/Balatro)
is a ~5% lottery ticket, not a plan. **Target the Backpack-Hero / Monster-Train
mid-tier.**

## 1. Viability — real market, harsh economics, rare breakouts

### The outcome distribution (the three tiers)
| Tier | Verified comparables | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Breakout (~5%)** | **Slay the Spire 2** ~4.6–5.3M copies / ~$92–108M *in EA* (3M week-one **official**; peak **574,638** concurrent); **Balatro** **$600K in first hours**, ~119K day-one units, **5M+** in <1yr | the *ceiling*, NOT the expectation |
| **Mid-tier (realistic-good)** | **Mechabellum** 1–2M owners ($14.99, mostly PvP); **Monster Train** 1.5M+ all-platforms (~500K–1M Steam-only); **Backpack Hero** ~577K copies / **~$7.6M gross → ~$1.35M *net*** ($19.99) | a sustainable living |
| **Niche floor** | **Kādomon: Hyper Auto Battlers** — **"Mixed," 66% / 536 reviews** | competent ≠ breakout; execution decides |

### Market size / trend
- **Steam roguelikes ≈ $400M in 2025, up ~80% YoY** (Alinea Analytics) — but the
  jump is **heavily inflated by the StS2 outlier** (verifier vote **2-1**, dissent
  flagged the distortion). Strong tailwind; **don't overweight it**.
- Larger **$3.8B–$4.8B** figures are *cross-platform* "market reports," **not**
  Steam-specific.

### Indie reality (sobering, verified 3-0)
- Indie = **98.9% of 13,007 Steam releases in 2024** → aggregate revenue spread
  thin across thousands.
- **Mean gross** rises with experience: 1st game **~$120K**, 2nd ~$168K, 3rd
  ~$209K. These are **MEANS; medians are far lower** (right-skewed).
- The "~$4B indie / 48% of Steam revenue" headline is **inflated by outliers**
  (Black Myth: Wukong alone ~$1B). Do **not** read it as near-AAA opportunity.
- **Long-tail (GameDiscoverCo):** a typical game earns **2.47–2.64× its
  first-week revenue** over year one; **only a 5% chance of a ~9.67× breakout.**
- **Gross ≠ take-home:** minus Steam's ~30%, tax, and refunds → you keep
  **roughly a third to a half.**

> **KILLED claims (do NOT cite):** "median indie ~$3,800" (0-3 ✗), "average indie
> ~$13,000" (0-3 ✗), "top-5% > $500K" (1-2 ✗). Unsupported / over-stated.

## 2. Competition & gaps — validated but contested

### Direct competitor — **Hive Blight** (the one to beat)
- Steam app **2886620**, dev Optizonion, **EA 2026-03-09**. *"A roguelite
  autobattler where you lead a team of insect warriors against a corrupting
  Fungal Threat."* Each run **allies two distinct insect clans** ≈ your
  collectible/mixable genelines.
- **"Very Positive," 93% / 252 reviews** (96% recent). Confirmed **3-0**.
- **Hivyss's differentiators (what Hive Blight LACKS):** pheromone *area-command*
  (HB = board/placement), a **hero "Royal" queen** (HB = none), an **in-battle
  swarm/hive economy**, **2.5D side-view** (HB = board).
- *Caveat: "near-identical" overstates it — premise matches, command system +
  economy differ (in your favor).*

### Adjacent competitors
- **Kādomon** (collectible-creature roguelike autobattler, 200+ creatures,
  synergy teams) — **"Mixed," 66%**. *Lesson: a competent niche entrant can ship
  to modest volume + divisive reception → execution/differentiation matter more
  than the genre tailwind.* (Confirmed 3-0.)
- **Mechabellum** — competitive **PvP** autobattler ("roguelike-adjacent"; the
  "roguelike" label overstates it).
- The roguelike-**deckbuilder** pocket is **crowded** (860+ on Steam).

### Theme demand (beyond Hive Blight)
- **RimWorld — Vanilla Factions Expanded: Insectoids 2** (Steam Workshop
  3309003431): **265,800 subscribers**, 274K visitors, 9.6K favorites. Adds
  hive-management, **breed insectoid castes**, insect-built structures, **jelly**,
  bosses, infestation events — *almost exactly Hivyss's design.* → strong
  *demand signal* for the insect-hive fantasy. **Caveat:** free-mod subs ≠ buyers.
- **Combat-shape demand:** **The Battle Cats** (mobile single-lane deploy-collect
  autobattler, since 2014, tens-of-millions+ downloads) + Stick War → the
  single-lane deploy-collect loop is **mass-proven fun**. **Caveat:** its *money*
  is mobile F2P **gacha** — **does NOT port** to a Steam-premium model.

### Whitespace verdict
Premise = **taken** (Hive Blight). Defensible whitespace = the **command verb
(pheromones) + the Royal hero + the in-battle swarm economy + the side-view tide.**
That's a real, ownable mechanical identity.

## 3. Commercial strategy

- **Pricing band: $14.99–$19.99** (Mechabellum $15; Bazaar & Backpack Hero $20).
- **Playbook:** Early Access + a **demo** + **Steam Next Fest** (the
  highest-leverage beat). Build **wishlists** ahead of launch.
- **Wishlist→sales benchmark UNKNOWN for this niche** — the StS2 figure was
  **refuted (1-2 ✗)** and removed. Treat wishlists as *"more is better; demo +
  Next Fest is the lever,"* not a precise target. (Open question below.)
- **Plan for steep post-launch decay** — **The Bazaar** ($19.99) peaked **17,136**
  concurrent and fell **~59% to ~6,992 in 3 months.** EA content cadence fights it.
- **Review quality > genre tailwind** — Kādomon's "Mixed" is the cautionary tale.
- This genre is **creator-driven** — StS, Balatro, and Backpack Battles grew via
  **streamers/content creators + the demo.**

## 4. Audience — the thin spot (honest gap)

The research **under-delivered** on audience sizing/channels (those claims didn't
survive verification — flagged as an **open question**). What IS solid:
- The roguelike-strategy / deckbuilder / autobattler crowd on Steam is **large,
  engaged, overlapping**; the **insect-hive theme** has demonstrated demand
  (Insectoids 2); the **combat shape** is mass-proven (Battle Cats / Stick War).
- **Channels:** content creators/streamers, the **Next Fest demo**, and the
  **roguelike + RimWorld-adjacent communities.**

## Caveats (the research's own)
- Most revenue/owner figures are **third-party estimates** (Alinea / SteamSpy /
  Gamalytic — ~2× error bands), not audited. Only MegaCrit's "3M week-one" is
  official.
- **Gross ≠ take-home** (~⅓–½ after Steam cut + tax + refunds).
- The **$400M / +80%** market figure is **inflated by StS2** (split vote).
- **Aggregate/mean ≠ individual forecast** — the median outcome sits *far below*
  every named comparable (survivorship/visibility bias).
- **Monster Train 1.5M = all-platforms;** Steam-only slice smaller (~500K–1M).
- **Time-sensitive:** concurrency/review/owner figures are point-in-time
  snapshots (2024–2026) and move.

## Open questions (unresolved by verified data)

   *this* niche (the StS2 benchmark was refuted).
2. The concrete role of a **demo + Next Fest** for the closest comparables

3. **Hive Blight's actual owner/sales estimates + price + retention curve** —
   the highest-value unknown (it sets Hivyss's realistic ceiling/floor).

## Strategic implications for Hivyss

**The niche is real and wanted — but Hive Blight got there first.** The job is no
longer "be the insect-hive autobattler"; it's **"out-differentiate and
out-execute Hive Blight."** And the differentiators *are the systems already in
design* (pheromones, the Royal + her positional kit, the swarm economy, the
side-view tide). Keep pointing those at the gap Hive Blight left open.

### Realistic target
**Backpack-Hero / Monster-Train mid-tier** (~$1–8M *gross*, sustainable). Treat
StS2/Balatro as the lottery, not the plan.

### Pros / Cons / Improvements (project self-assessment, 2026-06-13)

**PROS**
- **Clean, data-driven architecture** (registry seams, no tech debt, 654 tests) —
  rare for an indie; adding content = data, not code.
- **Genelines-as-win-paths** — a novel, replayable, RimWorld-validated hook.
- **Confirmed differentiators** vs the direct competitor (pheromone command,
  Royal hero, swarm economy, side-view).
- **Design rigor** (general/shared-slot/specific framework, worldbuilding
  discipline, the design bar) + **bespoke procedural art** = a distinct identity.
- **Three market validation signals** before launch (theme, combat shape, niche).

**CONS**
- **#1 risk still unproven: is the core loop FUN?** (Hive Blight proves the
  *genre* is fun — you must be *distinctly* fun.)
- **The *game* (the roguelike run) doesn't exist yet** — only a battle substrate;
  the run (node map + collect/mix genelines + escalation) is designed, not built.
  Only **2 genelines** built (α, β).
- **A liked competitor shipped first** (Hive Blight).
- **Scope keeps expanding** (incl. these design talks) vs. a solo dev → the gap
  between vision (huge) and built (a sandbox) is the scariest risk.
- **Foundational decisions still open** (lane vs open-field, perspective) — they
  gate everything.
- **No commercial presence yet** (no Steam page / wishlists / demo / creators).
- **Complexity risk** (currencies, castes, pheromones, genelines, towers) →
  onboarding burden. **Non-combat genelines** are the hardest balance bet.

**IMPROVEMENTS (prioritized)**
- 🔴 **Prove the core loop is fun** — a tight, playable vertical slice (one
  geneline + combat + economy + Royal + pheromones) in front of players.
- 🔴 **Lock the foundations** (lane/open-field + perspective); stop expanding scope.
- 🔴 **Build the actual run** (a thin roguelike loop: node map + 3–4 genelines + mix).
- 🟡 **Lean hard into the moat** (pheromones / Royal kit / swarm tide).
- 🟡 **Cut to an MVP** (~4–6 genelines; cosmology + node-capture + mix-meta = post-launch).
- 🟡 **Onboarding / clarity pass.**
- 🟢 **Start the commercial machine** — Steam page + wishlists + a Next-Fest demo +
  court creators. Price **$15–20**.
- 🟢 **System feedback/juice** — make cohesion + the systems legible on-screen
  (autobattlers live or die on watchability).

> **One-line verdict:** *Strong foundation, real differentiators, validated
> theme — but the game (the run) doesn't exist yet, the fun is unproven, and a
> competitor beat you to the premise. The risk isn't the idea; it's shipping.
> Build less, prove fun, ship something, point the moat at Hive Blight's gap.*
