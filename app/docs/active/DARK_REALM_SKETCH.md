## Context — what the Dark Realm is

The shadow mirror of the main map. Each of the first 22 Greek genelines has a
**1:1 Phoenician counterpart** (ψ/ω have none) — *"same archetype, dark
register,"* the unrefined/corrupted branch of the same primordial shard.
**Tier: +3 over the Greek twin** (Greek T0–6 → Phoenician T3–9; map data).
Universally hostile in v1 (no negotiation). Looting their genes grants power +
the dark stain (see economy below).

## Economy — Vyss / Dark Vyss (the elixir / dark-elixir pattern)

Don't add a 4th harvested currency. The "dark elixir" already exists as the
corruption axis — rename + reframe it:

| | term (working) | scope | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light essence | **Vyss** | battle (per-fight) | refined essence; spend on the command layer |
| Dark essence | **Dark Vyss** | run (persistent) | tainted essence; more potent but accrues the stain; replaces the generic "Corruption" axis |

- Vyss stays **universal substrate** (every geneline harvests essence of the
  fallen) — lore-consistent.
- In the Dark Realm the fallen are corrupted T3–6 vyssids, so the vyss you
  scavenge is **Dark Vyss** — richer (worth more / unlocks Phoenician-tier
  commands) but every spend nudges your persistent Dark Vyss up.
- Dark Vyss is the gate to the **True ending** (Dark Core / ת Taw needs it
  maxed) — power *and* the road to the truth, at the cost of what your hive
  becomes. (Bespoke name parked: *Dross* — "the impure residue of refining" —
  if "Dark Vyss" ever wants replacing.)
- Keep Dark Vyss a *register of vyss*, NOT a separate bar, unless Dark Realm
  playtest proves it needs a hard gate (weigh against the rejected 4th-currency
  cost). Visual: the violet-black register (below) separates it from Vyss.

## ב Beth — β Swarm's dark twin

**Identity:** *rot-black swarm — "units don't die: liquefy and re-spawn."*
Phoenician root *Beth = house/nest* → the nest gone septic. **Dark Skin, T3–6.**

**The corruption (2 genes that generate the whole roster):**
1. **Reformation** — β's units die *clean*; Beth's **liquefy into a rot-pool
   and re-form** unless cleansed. The pool *is* the unit.
2. **Lost agency** — the Phoenician signature (α can't-be-called-off, ε
   hijack-your-own). Beth's tide **seethes on its own timer** — you don't fully
   command its death or birth.

Throughline: **β SPENDS, Beth RECYCLES; β obeys, Beth seethes.** And the
+3-tier jump = +3 *sophistication* (tier = mechanical depth): each Beth unit is
its β twin's idea grown three levels more malignant.

that demands a different verb than every other fight.

## Art direction — the dark register of β's bodies

β's palette: bog-green primary `0x86a832`, maggot-flesh `0xdcd8ac`, **bruised
violet accent `0x5a3a72`**, bog shadow `0x28300c`. Beth **surfaces β's buried
violet from accent → dominant** (the Azula "blue fire" move — same body,
recolored to read "corrupted/elevated," not a new shape). Three unifying art
genes corrupt every β body coherently:

1. **Liquefaction** — where β bodies are taut/firm (Burster's taut skin,
   Hivespitter's planted gourd), Beth bodies **slump, drip, trail rot-strings**.
   Nothing is firm; everything is mid-melt.
2. **Surfaced violet** — green flesh → necrotic grey-violet; clean glows →
   sick violet pus-light. (Keep it green-shifted rot-violet, NOT clean
   Void-purple, or it misreads as void damage.)
3. **Open wounds over closed forms** — β's swelling-but-intact sacs/pustules
   become **burst, weeping, suppurating** ones. Ripe → ruptured.
   + Each body leaves a **wet puddle-shadow** (the ground is part of the unit —
   foreshadows Reformation). Motif-rhyme: β's clean egg/embryo orbs → Beth's
   festering rot-globs (same "containers of latent life," corrupted).

### Per-unit art twist (grounded in the real β draws)

- **Sludgeling** ← Swarmling: the oversized head **sags/half-melts**, body
  trails a rot-string to a puddle, the single eye is **milky/filmed**. Leg-blur
  → a smear of dribbling sludge.
- **Festerling** ← Maggotling: the back **spore-pustules → open weeping sores**
  venting violet; pale flesh necrotic and sloughing; the inchworm now **slithers
  through its own slime-trail**.
- **Bloatling** ← Burster: taut translucent abdomen → **over-ripe, blistered,
  sloshing** like a balloon about to split; the core glow is violet pus-light;
  it wobbles, doesn't sprint.
- **Ichorspewer** ← Hivespitter: the planted gourd is **rotted/caved-in** on
  one side; the spit-tube drips constantly into a pool; the throat-sac is a
  distended bladder of dark fluid.
- **Sloughback** ← Carrionling: the hunch is **half-skinless/raw** (sloughing =
  shedding skin), bone-speckles → exposed plate-gaps; rot-globs cling to it (the
  ones it drags); the beak drips.
- **Gorgelord** ← Swarmlord: baleen maw → **distended gullet** (half-digested
  bodies visible through translucent throat-skin); belly bulges with the eaten;
  orbiting flies thicken into a rot-cloud; **violet is now the whole body**.
- **Septlord** ← Broodlord: the egg-sac rack → a rack of **burst, suppurating
  wounds** leaking Sludgelings as fluid (not laying eggs); a walking abscess;
  antennae sloughed to stubs.
- **Mirequeen** ← Broodmother: instead of a dragged sac, her lower body has
  **dissolved into a spreading rot-pool** (becoming terrain); the brood-sac is a
  churning violet cauldron of half-formed bodies; the bone collar-frill stays
  intact (royal marker) while the rest sags. She doesn't move — the pool spreads.

### Effect recolor (Azula principle, presentation-layer only)

Today effect colors are fixed per damage type. A future **per-source color
override** lets the dark register recolor effects without touching the sim:
β bile = sickly yellow-green → Beth = **violet-black rot**. Rule: shift hue,
**preserve the effect's shape + readability** (still obviously "toxic/rot"),
and don't collide with another type's color (keep clear of clean Void-purple).
This same override is how β (bile, green) and the future θ Feeders (acid) stay
visually distinct while sharing the `toxic` damage type.

## The Angelfly order — the Dark-Veins enforcer (δ's dark twin)

> Ideation, 2026-06-12. Pairs with the §14 reframe + enforcement model in
> `STRATEGIC_DESIGN_SKETCH.md §9–10`.

A radiant **angel-INSECT** law-enforcer geneline — the **Phoenician dark twin
of δ Military** (δ's earthly disciplined order, corrupted into radiant
holy-terror). Polices its depth-band of the Dark Realm and **hunts the player
as a trespasser** (the "Marines vs intruder" structure).

- **Form (passes the reframed §14):** unmistakably a *bug* — chitin, six legs,
  compound eyes, antennae — with angelic radiance as *biological* flavor
  (bioluminescent halo-ring, translucent radiant wings, pale-gold chitin).
  Real precedent: lacewings, luna moths. NOT a haloed humanoid.
- **Rank ladder (fusion names):** **Angelfly → Archangelfly → Seraphid.** The
  apex Seraphid = the *biblically-accurate* angel (many-winged, many-eyed,
  blazing) — which is *already* insectoid + alien + deep-realm-horror. Three
  things converge in one creature.
- **Mechanic = suppression / nullification / ORDER** — strips buffs, cancels
  rule-breaking abilities, punishes corruption. The literal **anti-ψ** (ψ Psi =
  "the rule-breaker"; the Angelfly = the rule-*restorer*). A distinct, unclaimed
  control corner.
- **The inversion (the gem):** the *light* realm's authority looks **mundane**
  (δ's army); the *dark* realm's authority looks **divine** (radiant angel-bugs).
  The corrupted realm wears the mask of the holy. Fits the depth-scaling — deep
  realms look transcendent/uncanny, not generic.
