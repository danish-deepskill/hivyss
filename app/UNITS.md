# BUGATTLE - Unit Guide

## Tier Overview

| Tier | Power Level | Units |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **F** | Fodder | Grub |
| **E** | Basic | Mandible, Bombardier, Zephyr |
| **D** | Specialized | Locust, Aphid, Digger, Ember, Guardian, Hornet, Scarab |
| **C** | Elite | Mantis, Rhino, Beetle |
| **B** | Rare | Voltfly |

---

## F Tier

### Grub
> *Cheap disposable fodder. Send in waves to soak damage.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 55 |
| ATK | 12 |
| Speed | 1.6 |
| Range | 20 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 1.1/s |
| Cost | 15g |

**Ability:** None. Just a body.

**Strategy:** Spam them early to stall enemies while saving gold for stronger units. Great as meat shields for backline units like Locust or Mantis.

---

## E Tier

### Mandible
> *Reliable all-rounder. Decent HP, decent damage.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 140 |
| ATK | 32 |
| Speed | 1.2 |
| Range | 22 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 0.9/s |
| Cost | 40g |

**Ability:** None. Balanced stats across the board.

**Strategy:** Your bread-and-butter frontline fighter. Cost-efficient and tough enough to hold the line.

---

### Bombardier
> *Walking bomb. Hits all enemies in range and explodes on death.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 110 |
| ATK | 28 |
| Speed | 1.4 |
| Range | 22 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 0.85/s |
| Cost | 40g |

**Ability: Area Attack** — Every attack hits ALL enemies within range. On death, explodes for 35 damage to nearby enemies.

**Strategy:** Devastating against tightly packed enemy waves. Send behind a tank — even dying is useful thanks to the death explosion.

---

### Zephyr
> *Blazing fast scout. Fragile but reaches enemies before they reach you.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 40 |
| ATK | 20 |
| Speed | 3.5 |
| Range | 18 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 1.2/s |
| Cost | 40g |

**Ability:** None. Pure speed.

**Strategy:** Use as a rush unit to harass ranged enemies in the back. Also useful for quickly reaching the enemy base when it's unguarded. Glass cannon — don't expect them to survive long.

---

## D Tier

### Locust
> *Ranged attacker. Stays back and chips away safely.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 75 |
| ATK | 42 |
| Speed | 0.9 |
| Range | 90 (ranged) |
| ATK Rate | 0.75/s |
| Cost | 50g |

**Ability:** Ranged attack from a safe distance.

**Strategy:** Place behind melee units. Decent damage output but dies fast if enemies get close. Pairs well with Guardian's damage reduction aura.

---

### Aphid
> *Battlefield medic. Low damage but keeps your army alive.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 120 |
| ATK | 8 |
| Speed | 0.8 |
| Range | 60 (ranged) |
| ATK Rate | 0.6/s |

| Cost | 50g |

**Ability: Heal** — Every 2 seconds, heals the nearest wounded ally for 20 HP.

**Strategy:** Deploy behind your frontline. One Aphid can keep a tank like Scarab or Beetle alive significantly longer. The healing adds up fast in prolonged fights.

---

### Digger
> *Tunnel specialist. Burrows underground and surfaces behind enemy lines.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 100 |
| ATK | 25 |
| Speed | 2.5 |
| Range | 20 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 1.0/s |
| Cost | 55g |

**Ability: Burrow** — Goes underground for 2 seconds on spawn, moving 1.5x faster and untargetable. Surfaces behind the enemy front line.

**Strategy:** Bypasses the enemy frontline to attack squishy backline units like ranged attackers and healers. The surprise factor is its biggest strength.

---

### Ember
> *Fire bug. Sets enemies ablaze with lingering burn damage.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 80 |
| ATK | 40 |
| Speed | 1.3 |
| Range | 30 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 0.8/s |
| Cost | 65g |

**Ability: Burn (AOE)** — Attacks spread burn to up to 3 nearby enemies. Burn deals 4 damage every 0.5 seconds for 8 seconds (64 total per target). Burn refreshes on re-hit but doesn't stack.

**Strategy:** Incredible against clumped enemies. A single Ember can burn 3 targets simultaneously. The burn keeps dealing damage even after Ember dies. Best paired with units that can keep enemies grouped together.

---

### Guardian
> *Protective aura tank. Reduces damage taken by nearby allies.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 300 |
| ATK | 15 |
| Speed | 0.7 |
| Range | 25 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 0.5/s |
| Cost | 70g |

**Ability: Damage Aura** — All allies within range take 20% less damage.

**Strategy:** The ultimate support tank. Place in the middle of your army to shield everyone. The 20% damage reduction is huge in longer fights. Stacks with healing from Aphid for maximum survivability.

---

### Hornet
> *Berserker wasp. Gets more dangerous the closer it is to death.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 140 |
| ATK | 55 (up to 110) |
| Speed | 2.2 |
| Range | 20 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 1.3/s |
| Cost | 100g |

**Ability: Berserk** — ATK increases as HP drops. Below 50% HP: 1.5x ATK (82). Below 25% HP: 2x ATK (110).

**Strategy:** Fast and deadly. Send in groups — as they take damage, their output skyrockets. Pairs amazingly with Aphid who can keep them hovering in the rage zone without dying.

---

### Scarab
> *Living fortress. Massive HP pool to absorb punishment.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 400 |
| ATK | 18 |
| Speed | 0.6 |
| Range | 26 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 0.5/s |
| Cost | 85g |

**Ability:** None. Pure bulk.

**Strategy:** Your premier tank. Deploy first to soak enemy attacks while damage dealers work behind it. With 400 HP, it takes a long time to chew through. Add Guardian + Aphid for a nearly unkillable frontline.

---

## C Tier

### Mantis
> *Long-range sniper. Massive damage from across the battlefield.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 100 |
| ATK | 120 |
| Speed | 0.3 |
| Range | 200 (sniper) |
| ATK Rate | 0.25/s |
| Cost | 85g |

**Ability: Piercing Shot** — Attacks hit up to 2 enemies in a line. First target takes full damage, second takes 50%.

**Strategy:** Highest single-hit damage in the game. Very slow movement and attack speed — needs a strong frontline to protect it. One well-placed Mantis behind a Scarab can delete enemies before they get close.

---

### Rhino
> *Charging ram. Smashes enemies backward on hit.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 280 |
| ATK | 45 |
| Speed | 1.0 |
| Range | 24 (melee) |
| ATK Rate | 0.7/s |
| Cost | 95g |

**Ability: Knockback** — Each attack rams the target backward. Enemies are staggered during knockback (can't move or attack while sliding).

**Strategy:** Excellent crowd control. Keeps enemies pushed away from your base and disrupts their attacks. The stagger effect is key — a Rhino can effectively stunlock a single target. Great against slow heavy enemies.

---

### Beetle
> *Armored poison tank. Shrugs off damage and poisons on hit.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 450 |
| ATK | 30 |
| Speed | 0.7 |
| Range | 55 (ranged) |
| ATK Rate | 0.55/s |
| Cost | 120g |

**Ability: Shield + Poison** — Takes 50% reduced damage for the first 3 seconds after spawning. Attacks apply poison (5 damage/second for 3 seconds).

**Strategy:** The tankiest offensive unit. The spawn shield lets it absorb an initial burst, and the poison stacks up on anything it hits. Ranged attack means it can deal damage from relative safety.

---

## B Tier

### Voltfly
> *Electric dragonfly. Chain lightning arcs between multiple enemies.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 240 |
| ATK | 55 |
| Speed | 1.1 |
| Range | 85 (ranged) |
| ATK Rate | 0.8/s |
| Cost | 130g |

**Ability: Chain Lightning** — Attacks chain to up to 3 enemies within range. Damage falls off per chain (100% / 70% / 40%). Each target has a 25% chance to be stunned for 0.6 seconds. Every 4th attack is an Overcharge dealing 2x damage.

**Strategy:** The best unit against swarms. Chain lightning melts groups of enemies, and the stun can lock down multiple targets at once. Time your deployment for when enemy waves are clustered. The Overcharge every 4th hit is devastating.
