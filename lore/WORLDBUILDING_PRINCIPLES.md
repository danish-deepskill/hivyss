# Worldbuilding Principles — the discipline for touching the lore

> **Why this exists:** the cosmology in `HIVYSS.md` *accreted without rigor*
> ("random worldbuilding creation"). It has strong bones (the body-as-anatomy
> metaphor, reproduction-as-planetary-memory) but also classic worldbuilder
> failure modes. These are the **rules** — the steering, the same way the
> geneline-budget rules steer roster design. Lore is **frozen for the MVP**
> (`REQUIREMENTS §1`); apply these whenever it IS touched, and to all
> deep-future content (genelines ε–ω, the realms, the Dark Realm/Beth sketch).

---

## The five rules

1. **Mechanical-truth.** No cosmological claim survives unless a mechanic makes
   it *true in play*. Lore that the game contradicts is purple prose, and it
   poisons trust in the rest. *(Live violation: `HIVYSS §1` "every death is
   heredity… the next generation begins closer" — but v1 resets per run. See
   the open reconcile below.)*

2. **Canon horizon.** Design *deeply* only what ships. Everything past it is
   **intentionally-thin myth** — named, gestured at, deliberately unresolved.
   Unbuilt content is *negative space*, not debt. (`HIVYSS §17 "The Unchosen"`
   is the instinct; this makes it the rule.)

3. **Inference over exposition.** The player *assembles* the cosmology; the doc
   and the game never hand it over. The current lore over-explains on page one
   (it tells you the Core is lonely, that *you are vyss rehearsing*) — the
   opposite of the cited Qud/Souls method. Withhold; let the player do the work.

4. **Numerology emerges, never dictates.** Let each system pick the count that
   serves *play*. If 7 falls out naturally, lovely — but never crush UX to hit a
   mystical number. *(Live violation: rep tiers crushed 9→7 "to mirror the
   7-fold primordial structure" — design serving numerology instead of play.)*

5. **Earns its place in PLAY, not on the page.** The ruthless test for every
   element. If it only exists in the lore bible and never in a player's hands,
   it's not done — it's decoration.

## Scope is a BUFFET, not a gauntlet (the agentic-era reframe)

The "you can't build 60 genelines" worry is dead: agentic authoring + the
**generative tree** (7 primordials → 24 Greek deviations → 22 Phoenician
corruptions → 7 Coptic preservations) make the volume buildable. So:

- The 60 genelines are a **horizontal choice-menu** (Dota's 128 heroes —
  breadth = replayability), **not a vertical corridor** players must traverse.
  *"Nobody sees all of it"* is the **point**, not a failure — for the buffet.
- Discipline still binds the **gauntlet**: author *deeply* only the genelines
  the *required descent path* forces players through. The rest are buffet-only.
- **Cheap authoring inverts the risk** — from *"you'll never finish"* to
  *"you'll generate slop that dilutes."* So the budget/bar discipline matters
  **more**, not less. The agent is the engine; this discipline is the steering.

---

## Distinctness: shared effects OK, shared IDENTITY never (the Dota rule)

Dota ships 128 distinct heroes by **sharing effects** (20+ have stuns) but
**never identities** (there is one Pudge). So:

- Two genelines may use the same mechanic / damage type. They may **not**
  duplicate the same *identity corner* (army-model · economy · death-stance ·
  verb — `VISION §6`).
- β *uses* toxic; it is not *the toxic faction* (that's θ Feeders' wrapper).
  Acid/necro/air/tunnel are reserved as **identity** corners, not
  mechanic-exclusivity. (Reservations: `app/docs/mvp/GENELINES.md §1.0`.)

## Improve freely WITHIN the scaffold (sketches are not canon)

Every per-geneline theme/hook in `HIVYSS §7` is tagged **"(proposed)"** and
listed in `§12` OPEN. Treat them as **first drafts to beat**, held to the
design bar — not locks. What IS load-bearing (don't break): the counts, the
Phoenician 1:1 mirror, ψ/ω's specials, primordial ancestry → faction, and the
layer-depth → tier/weirdness gradient. **Hold the structural scaffold; treat
all content as canvas.**

---

## The design bar (cross-ref)

Lore quality = the same bar as art: each geneline is an **ecosystem with a
thesis** (`HIVYSS §6`), not a hook + reskins. Name-true, fresh-sheet,
motif-rhyming (β Swarm / Fetid Pool is the benchmark). See the design-bar
memory + `GENELINES.md`.

---

## Open reconciles (the content fixes the rules surface)

Priority order — these are where the lore currently *fails its own rules*:

1. **The false-heredity line (rule 1).** `HIVYSS §1`. Either **build** the thin
   cross-run inheritance that makes "every death is heredity" true (a trait, a
   remembered form, a scar carried forward), **or rewrite** the line to match
   the reset reality. Highest-priority coherence fix.
2. **Over-explained premise (rule 3).** Move "you are vyss rehearsing" from a
   stated premise to a player-assembled revelation.
3. **Numerology-crushed rep tiers (rule 4).** Re-justify the tier count by
   readability, not by the number 7.
4. **The Core-designed-everything trope (deflation risk).** Make the designer
   *imperfect/failing* so player deviation is genuinely unplanned, not theater.
