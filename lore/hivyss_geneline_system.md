# Hivyss — The Geneline System (48)

Engine-first. Every geneline is **Element + Theme**: the *Theme* carries the economy hook (load-bearing, set by the layer); the *Element* is the flavor. Nothing here is a costume.

---

## 1. The Engine (three rules)

Hivyss is a **metabolism**, not a list of elements:

> nectar → larva → body → **corpse → vyss** → command → more death → more vyss

1. **Vertical position = deviation from the honest loop.**
   - **Surface (0)** = honest. Balanced, vyss-neutral.
   - **Down (Abyss)** = *dissolution* → **floods vyss**. Cost: degrades colony integrity.
   - **Up (Celestial)** = *abstraction* → **starves vyss**. Cost: can't fuel a swarm; wins by control/tempo.
2. **Byte-tier = sophistication** (see §2). Distance from surface = how advanced the vyssid is.
3. **geneline = Element × Theme (role forced by position) × Tier.** Creativity is the *collision* of an element with a role it isn't clichéd for.

**Litmus test:** strip a cell's draw + element-noun. Still distinct from its neighbors above/below? Load-bearing. Only paint left? Costume — merge or move it.

---

## 2. The Byte-Ladder (sophistication axis)

Like data sizes. Higher tier = more advanced / complex / smarter / stronger.

`V (byte) → kV → MV → GV → TV → PV → EV → ZV → YV → RV → QV (quetta) → HV (Hivyss, apex)`

**Distance from surface = tier.** Surface units are byte-simple; the abyss ladders up over ten rungs, the celestial climbs steeper over six. Only the two capstones — **dei** (top) and **sampi** (bottom) — reach **HV**. The lost-letter "apex strain" in each layer sits at the *top* of that layer's tier-window.

## 3. The Letter Lore — deadness = distance

- **Living Greek (24: α–ω) = the Known World.** Surface + the depths the colony understands.
- **Dead scripts (24) = the Lost Realms**, the further out the deader:
  - **Obsolete Greek (7):** digamma, heta, stigma, san, koppa, sampi, sho
  - **Coptic (7):** shei, fei, khei, hori, gangia, shima, dei
  - **Gothic (10):** faihu, iggws, nauths, winja, aihvus, qairthra, hwair, thiuth, urus, othala

## 5. The Roster — all 48

Format: **letter — Element + Theme** · hook · *playstyle*. (★ = lost-letter apex strain, top of the layer's tier-window.)

### SURFACE — honest loop (V–kV)

**0 Primal — The Bloomfield**
- **α alpha — Earth + Primal** *(built)*: cohesion herd, tough, holds line, low vyss, snowballs together · *anchor/phalanx*
- **ξ xi — Honey + Forager**: bonus nectar, weak combat; the macro engine · *economic investment*
- **ο omicron — Clay + Generalist**: flat stats, no hook; the honest baseline · *balanced starter*
- **faihu — Amber + Hoarder ★**: stockpiles nectar, then releases a burst command-discount · *economic tempo*

### DESCENT — swarm & resource (floods vyss)

**−1 Swarm — The Warren (kV–MV)**
- **β beta — Acid + Swarm** *(built)*: cheapest bodies, huge vyss-on-death, melts; corpses leave acid that burns whoever stands on them · *zerg flood*
- **γ gamma — Stone + Fortress**: cheap but tanky blockers that simply hold the line · *wall of bodies*
- **δ delta — Spark + Replicant**: corpses spawn a weaker larva; spark chains on death · *self-replenishing tide*
- **ε epsilon — Resin + Hatchery**: incubates extra swarm mid-fight, cheaper than command · *production engine*
- **ζ zeta — Ember + Frenzy**: gains speed/damage as nearby allies die · *snowball-on-death*
- **iggws — Hornet + Raid-Swarm ★**: fast swarm that bypasses the front to strike the back line · *aggressive flanker*

**−2 Carrion — The Midden (MV–GV)**
- **η eta — Rot + Carrion-feeder**: gains stats per corpse on the field · *scales with carnage*
- **θ theta — Bone + Recycler**: converts corpses to bonus vyss · *economy amplifier*
- **ι iota — Flesh + Scavenger-swarm**: spawns from **enemy** corpses · *parasitic numbers*
- **κ kappa — Gas + Bloat**: its corpses emit a damaging rot-cloud · *area attrition off death*

**−3 Blight — The Mire (GV–TV)**
- **λ lambda — Venom + Plaguebearer**: stacking poison DoT, weak burst · *attrition DPS*
- **μ mu — Spore + Fungal**: contaminated tiles damage all — **including your own blooms** · *terrain denial with self-cost*
- **ν nu — Mold + Contagion**: a debuff that spreads unit-to-unit · *viral*
- **nauths — Salt + Wither ★**: withers an enemy nectar bloom, denying their macro economy · *economic blight*

**−4 Parasite — The Hollows (TV–PV)**
- **π pi — Oil + Leech**: attaches to an enemy, siphons its energy/income to you · *sabotage*
- **ρ rho — Blood + Brood-parasite**: kills spawn your copy from the host body · *convert-on-kill*
- **winja — Cordyceps + Puppeteer ★**: *seizes control* of one living enemy unit, not just kills it · *mind-control theft*

**−5 Necros — The Crypt (PV–EV)**
- **σ sigma — Ash + Reanimator**: raises corpses into decaying revenants (trade their vyss for bodies) · *corpse → army*
- **τ tau — Bone + Bonewright**: builds a persistent undead wall from bones · *fortify off death*
- **aihvus — Cinder + Pyre-lord ★**: cremates corpses for a big energy burst instead of raising them · *vyss → power spike*

**−6 Corruption — The Rot-Ruin (EV–ZV)**
- **digamma — Plague + Suborn**: converts a living enemy to your side, temporarily (decays) · *theft of force*
- **koppa — Spore + Contagion-lord**: corrupted/killed units corrupt their neighbors · *cascade*
- **qairthra — Rust + Corroder ★**: permanently corrodes enemy Elites' stats · *anti-elite corrosion*

**−7 Aberration — The Warp (ZV–YV)**
- **stigma — Flesh-warp + Nightmare**: induces fear (enemies flee, lose cohesion); self-mutates · *psychological disruption*
- **hwair — Mutagen + Aberrant ★**: randomly mutates *your own* units into stronger/buggier forms · *high-ceiling mutation*

**−8 Chaos — The Maelstrom (YV–RV)**
- **heta — Static + Entropy**: every action rolls powerful/backfire; scrambles enemy commands · *gamble/disruption*
- **thiuth — Discord + Unmaker ★**: briefly inverts a battlefield rule (e.g. swaps strata) · *rule-scrambling*

**−9 Void — The Soul-Well (RV–QV)**
- **san — Soul + Reaper**: its kills yield no corpse/vyss to anyone, but it harvests the slain's soul = the vyss they'd have given, straight to you · *economic denial + theft*
- **urus — Null + Devourer ★**: erases a unit with **no** death-trigger and no corpse · *counters revives & death-bombs*

**−10 Hell — The Inferno-Core (QV–HV)** *(capstone — mirror of dei)*
- **sampi — Hellfire + Demon Sovereign**: once per run, impose a catastrophic global law (e.g. all deaths yield double vyss, but hive integrity drains each turn); one apex demon · *down-pole god-unit*

### ASCENT — control & tempo (starves vyss)

**+1 Drift — The Canopy (kV–GV)**
- **υ upsilon — Wind + Skirmisher**: air-only striker, ignores the ground line · *harass*
- **φ phi — Pollen + Drifter**: drifts spores that slow/weaken a lane segment · *zone softening*
- **χ chi — Sound + Signal**: amplifies pheromone commands (cheaper, wider); no bodies · *force-multiplier*

**+2 Husk (Lunar) — Molting Grounds (GV–PV)**
- **ψ psi — Frost + Tidal**: power waxes & wanes on a cycle · *rhythmic windows*
- **ω omega — Chitin + Molt**: leaves a hardened husk-wall on death instead of a corpse · *defensive attrition*
- **othala — Relic-bone + Heirloom ★**: inherits a buff from each ally that dies near it — grows from your dead *without* raising them · *legacy accumulator*

**+3 System — The Glitch-Lattice (PV–ZV)**
- **sho — Phantom + Spectral**: intangible, ignores blocking, leaves no corpse · *infiltration*
- **gangia — Glitch + Datacorrupt**: randomly duplicates/despawns its units; can copy one enemy def · *high-variance value*
- **shima — Logic + Hivemind**: buffs scale with the number of **distinct** genelines fielded · *synergy engine*

**+4 Space — The Rift (ZV–RV)**
- **khei — Aether + Blink**: teleports units past the line, bypassing the push · *tempo/flank*
- **hori — Chrono + Temporal**: borrows future nectar now / slows enemy maturation · *tempo manipulation*

**+5 Galaxy — Starfield Drift (RV–QV)**
- **shei — Solar + Radiant**: persistent burn-aura; its kills ash out → reduced vyss for everyone · *zone denial*
- **fei — Gravity + Astral**: pulls enemy units out of formation, bends the push · *displacement control*

**+6 Celestial — The Empyrean (QV–HV)** *(capstone — mirror of sampi)*
- **dei — Light + Cosmic Sovereign**: once per battle, rewrite one global rule for the rest of the fight; zero vyss, catastrophic nectar, one body · *up-pole god-unit*

## 6. The Up/Down Mirror

Pair by distance from surface and they rhyme — one engine, not two lists. The poles are exact twins:

| | Ascent (abstraction) | Descent (dissolution) |
|---|---|---|
| Pole | **dei — Light + Cosmic** | **sampi — Hellfire + Demonic** |
| Both | width-1, HV-tier, once-per-run, rewrite a global rule |

## 7. Build Order (don't drown in 48)

Your bet is *"is the swarm combat fun."* Answer it with **three layers**: −1, 0, +1.

1. **Ship the surface ring** (~10 genelines: Drift, Primal, Swarm). All low-tier, honest economy, three strata.
2. **Prove the deviation axis feels different up vs down** — starving vyss (Drift) vs flooding it (Swarm). If yes, the engine works and the rest are interpolations.
3. **Ladder outward one ring at a time** as run-depth unlocks. The HV capstones (dei, sampi) are endgame spectacle the engine *generates* — don't hand-paint them first.

**Sanderson's rule:** deepen what you have before adding new. Six maximally-spread genelines teach you more than 48 costumes.
